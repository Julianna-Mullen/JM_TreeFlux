# JM_TreeFlux

This is Julianna's Summer SULI research project, examining tree stem 
fluxes from the TEMPEST 4 Flood.

As part of the Department of Energy’s Science Undergraduate Laboratory Internship (SULI) program, I worked with the Coastal Observations, Mechanisms, and Predictions Across Systems and Scales (COMPASS) group at Pacific Northwest National Laboratory’s Joint Global Change Research Institute on the Terrestrial Ecosystem Manipulation to Probe the Effects of Storm Treatments (TEMPEST) project. My research examined how flooding treatments influence methane (CH₄) and carbon dioxide (CO₂) fluxes from coastal tree stems using data collected from the Smithsonian Environmental Research Center.
